# V25.1 Theory Expansion

This build completes the Theory & Method revision while preserving the Adam Saffin and Juan Patricio case-page prose. The case pages retain only the shared title-alignment and stylesheet-version updates.

## Theory visuals

1. **From Theft to Recursive Dispossession** appears in **Dispossession**. It contrasts the familiar account of theft with a recursive account in which dispossession helps produce property.
2. **Ante-Possession: Relations to Ownership** appears under **A Central Question**. It presents ante-possession as before, against, and apposite to ownership.
3. **Recursive Dispossession: How the Cycle Expands** appears in **Trans-Atlantic Slave Trade**. Its evenly spaced cycle is Capture → Propertization → Capitalized → Expansion.
4. **The Maní Land Treaty Map (1557): Space as Relation** appears in **Cartography**. Its thin screenshot border is removed, and restrained contrast and sharpening improve legibility without altering the document’s content.

The diagrams are native HTML and CSS, use the established paper, ink, rule, and seal-wax palette, and collapse into a vertical reading order on narrow screens. Diagram stages and figure labels use Roman numerals. The Ante-Possession hub is a horizontal ellipse so its title remains on one line.

## Prose

The approved explanatory material is integrated into the existing Theory & Method prose and uses the site’s normal text color. The revision balances the implications of the Juan Patricio and Adam cases, keeps scholarly attribution in citations and vocabulary metadata, and avoids em dashes throughout the theory page.
