## Reading-derived and archival images

- The Indigenous-geographies comparison map is retained and credited as an adaptation from Lisa Brooks and Jenny Davis, “Map 1. The Indigenous Northeast: A Network of Waterways,” in *The Common Pot* (2008).
- The Maní Land Treaty Map is credited to Gaspar Antonio Chi, 1557, Latin American Library, Tulane University.
- The historical Yucatán comparison layer is identified as a detail from Antonio Espinosa’s ca. 1910 map of the peninsula.
- The Juan Patricio cover and landing-page hero are identified with the 1696 *compulsa* held in the Archivo General de Indias.
- The Adam manuscript photograph and cropped case-index banners remain identified as project research images when a more specific scan or photographer record was not preserved.
- Contributor portraits are credited courtesy of the individuals depicted.

## Link-check qualification

Automated requests to the Kazanjian article DOI destinations returned access-control responses, but the citations and DOI records were separately checked against the official Oxford University Press and Duke University Press records. This is an automated-access limitation, not evidence of a broken citation.

## Retention decision

No images were removed. Reading-derived or project-supplied assets with incomplete scan-level provenance were retained at the project editor’s direction and described without inventing unsupported attribution.
